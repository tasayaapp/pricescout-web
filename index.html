The Problem

Your grocery budget shouldn't be a guessing game

Between classes, labs, and everything else — who has time to drive around comparing prices?

😓
The old way

You either pick the closest store and pay more, or spend your Sunday afternoon driving to three different places hoping to save a few dollars. You never really know if you got the best price.

✨
With PriceScout

Scan a barcode or snap a receipt and instantly see prices at every store near campus. The whole community keeps prices updated — so you always have the freshest data, not yesterday's flyer.
